How to use Quaver
Click a staff
Place a note there. In the full score, the staff you click becomes the part you are writing.
Click / drag a note
Click selects it. Drag it up or down to change its pitch. Shift+click selects a range; double-click selects the whole bar.
Chords
Turn on Chord mode, then click above or below a note. Or select a note and press Shift+A–G.
A–G
Type a note by letter. 1–5 pick whole to sixteenth, T triplets.
R . + - N
Rest, dot, sharp, flat, natural. These apply to the next note you place.
Ctrl/Cmd+C V X D
Copy, paste, cut, and repeat the selection.
L / K
Type lyrics / chord symbols for the selected note. Space or Enter moves to the next note.
S
Slur the selected notes.
Space
Play or stop from the selected note. Turn on Loop to repeat the selected bars.
Esc
Stop the music, even while you're typing in a box. The red Stop button at the bottom of the screen also stays visible while music plays.
Sonnet
Sonnet reads your score, researches it, suggests markings, and can write new music (harmonies, bass lines, drum grooves, arrangements) that you add with one click. Your key goes only to Anthropic. Don't save it on a shared computer.
